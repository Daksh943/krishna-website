The Divine Story

Krishna's Life Journey

01
Birth in Mathura
Born in a prison cell to Devaki and Vasudeva, Krishna's divine birth was heralded by celestial music and flowers. The shackles fell away as the divine child appeared at midnight on Ashtami.
02
Childhood in Gokul
Raised by Yashoda and Nanda, little Kanha enchanted all of Gokul with his mischief — stealing butter, dancing on Kaliya serpent, and showing his cosmic form to his mother.
03
Vrindavan — The Forest of Love
In the sacred groves of Vrindavan, Krishna played his flute and performed the Rasa Leela with the gopis. Every moment in Vrindavan was a divine poem of love and devotion.
04
Govardhan Hill
When Indra sent devastating rains to punish Vrindavan, Krishna lifted the mighty Govardhan Hill on his little finger for seven days, sheltering all beings beneath it.
05
Return to Mathura
Answering his divine duty, Krishna returned to Mathura, slew the tyrant Kamsa, and restored Devaki and Vasudeva to their rightful place. Justice and dharma were restored.
06
Dwarka — Kingdom of the Sea
Krishna established Dwarka, the golden city rising from the ocean, and ruled as king — an ideal statesman, strategist, and friend to all who sought his shelter.
07
Mahabharata
The pivotal war of Kurukshetra — where Krishna served as Arjuna's charioteer. Through his divine counsel, the world witnessed dharma prevail over adharma in the greatest battle ever fought.
08
Bhagavad Gita
On the battlefield of Kurukshetra, with Arjuna overwhelmed by grief, Krishna revealed the eternal wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita — 700 verses that have guided humanity for millennia.

Divine Philosophy

Krishna's Teachings

धर्म
Dharma
Perform your duty with sincerity and without selfish motive. Your role in the cosmic order is sacred.
Each soul is born with a unique dharma — a sacred purpose. When we live in alignment with our true nature and duty, we contribute to the harmony of the universe.
कर्म
Karma
Focus entirely on your actions rather than the results. The fruits will follow naturally from pure effort.
The law of karma is perfect justice. Every thought, word, and action creates ripples in the fabric of existence. Act from love, not from fear of outcome.
भक्ति
Bhakti
Devotion creates the deepest connection with the divine. Love is the fastest path to liberation.
Bhakti yoga — the path of devotion — is available to all, regardless of learning or status. When the heart overflows with love for the divine, the veil of illusion dissolves.
शांति
Peace
Steady the mind and remain balanced in joy and sorrow, gain and loss. Equanimity is true strength.
Peace is not the absence of challenge but the presence of perfect stillness within it. The enlightened soul remains like a lamp unflickered by wind — luminous and steady.
Sacred Celebrations

Divine Festivals

🪔
Janmashtami
The grand celebration of Lord Krishna's birth. Devotees fast, sing, dance, and break the Dahi Handi at the stroke of midnight — the divine moment when Krishna entered our world.
Bhadra Ashtami · August–September
🎨
Holi
The festival of colors celebrates Krishna and Radha's divine playfulness in Vrindavan. Devotees drench each other in vibrant hues, celebrating the victory of love and spring's arrival.
Phalguna Purnima · February–March
⛰️
Govardhan Puja
Commemorating Krishna's miraculous lifting of Govardhan Hill to protect Vrindavan from Indra's wrath. Devotees build mountains of food and perform circumambulation with devotion.
Kartik Shukla Pratipada · October–November
🌸
Radhashtami
The sacred appearance day of Shrimati Radharani — the divine consort of Krishna. Her love for Krishna is considered the highest form of devotion and the embodiment of pure bhakti.
Bhadra Shukla Ashtami · August–September
